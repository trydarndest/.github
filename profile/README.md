### Hi there 👋, we're Darndest

#### An independent software studio in New York City, making software that respects your time and attention.

We design and build a short list of apps for Apple platforms, with the patience to get the details right. Most software is built to take up as much of your day as it can. We build the opposite: focused tools you open with a purpose, use well, and close.

**What we do**

- **Design**: interface and interaction design grounded in Apple's Human Interface Guidelines.
- **Engineering**: native apps in Swift and SwiftUI, with a small codebase and a shorter dependency list.
- **Audio & content**: original narration, sound design and session writing, produced in-house.

**Our first product: [Journalcast](https://journalcast.app)**

A guided journaling app that pairs narrated audio sessions with background music, so you never face a blank page alone. [Download it on the App Store](https://journalcast.app/download).

**How we work**

- **Your data is yours.** We collect the minimum an app needs, say plainly what it is, and never sell it.
- **No engagement traps.** No streak guilt, no manufactured urgency, no notifications built to pull you back in.
- **Honest pricing.** You always know what you're paying for and how to stop.
- **Accessible by default.** Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, sensible contrast and reduced motion ship in the first release, not a later one.
